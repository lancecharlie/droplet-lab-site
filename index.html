Droplet Lab

A quiet ink-wash puzzle: lead each drop of colour home.

Support

Questions, a level you think is wrong, or anything else: write to synfuel.cuffs_7y@icloud.com. Please mention the level number and your device.

How to play

  • Tap a square next to a droplet to draw the droplet onto it.
  • Droplets drawn onto the same square merge into one.
  • Lead each colour into the ring that wants it, at the amount written in the ring.
  • Undo and Restart are always free. Match the level's par for three stars.
  • Colour-blind players can turn on Patterns on the home screen: every colour then carries its own pattern.

支持

有问题、觉得哪一关不对，或者别的事，请写信到 synfuel.cuffs_7y@icloud.com，注明关卡号和设备型号。

怎么玩

  • 点一下液滴旁边的格子，液滴就会被引过去。
  • 被引到同一格的液滴会融成一滴。
  • 把每种颜色送进要它的环，量要和环里的数字一样。
  • 撤销和重来不限次数。走到最优步数拿三星。
  • 分不清颜色的玩家可以在主页打开「纹样」，每种颜色会带上自己的纹样。
