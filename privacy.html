Privacy Policy

Droplet Lab · effective 17 November 2026

Droplet Lab does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The game has no accounts and makes no network requests.
  • Your progress (stars, daily results and settings) is saved only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.
  • The game contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking code.
  • If you share a daily result, the text goes to the app you pick in the system share sheet; the game itself sends nothing.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Contact: synfuel.cuffs_7y@icloud.com.

隐私政策

液滴实验室不收集、不存储、不分享任何个人数据。

  • 游戏没有账号，不发出任何网络请求。
  • 进度（星星、每日一题成绩和设置）只保存在你的设备上，删除 app 即一并删除。
  • 游戏没有广告，也没有统计或追踪代码。
  • 如果你分享每日一题的成绩，文字会交给你在系统分享面板里选的 app，游戏本身不发送任何东西。

本政策如有变更，新版本会以新日期发布在本页。联系：synfuel.cuffs_7y@icloud.com。
